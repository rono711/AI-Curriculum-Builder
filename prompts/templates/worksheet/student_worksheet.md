## Requirements

Create a classroom-ready worksheet suitable for Australian Curriculum students.

The worksheet must contain:

1. Student Name
2. Class
3. Date

---

### Learning Intention

Provide one concise learning intention.

---

### Success Criteria

Provide 3–5 student-friendly success criteria.

---

### Warm-up

Provide three short review questions.

---

### Guided Practice

Provide five scaffolded questions that gradually increase in difficulty.

---

### Independent Practice

Provide eight questions requiring students to apply the learning independently.

Include a mixture of:

- Multiple choice
- Short answer
- Worked calculations (where appropriate)
- Problem solving

---

### Challenge

Provide one extension activity suitable for higher-achieving students.

---

### Reflection

Include three reflection questions such as:

- What did I learn today?
- What did I find difficult?
- What would I like more practice with?

---

### Teacher Notes

Do NOT include answers.

Do NOT include marking guides.

Produce only the student worksheet.

Format using clean Markdown with clear headings.
