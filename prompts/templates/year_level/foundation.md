# Foundation Year Learners

The lesson is for Foundation Year students.

Design every aspect of the lesson to suit learners who are typically beginning formal schooling Elaboration:
{{ELABORATION}}.

## Characteristics

Students are generally:

- learning through play,
- learning through movement,
- developing oral language,
- building early literacy,
- developing early numeracy,
- learning routines,
- developing attention and self-regulation.

Learning should be enjoyable, practical and highly engaging.

---

## Lesson Design

Keep explanations short.

Use:

- demonstrations,
- modelling,
- songs where appropriate,
- games,
- visuals,
- storytelling,
- movement,
- repetition.

Avoid long teacher explanations.

---

## Vocabulary

Introduce only a small number of new words.

Explain every unfamiliar word using simple language.

Reinforce vocabulary throughout the lesson.

---

## Activities

Activities should be:

- practical,
- visual,
- interactive,
- hands-on,
- achievable.

Children should be actively involved throughout the lesson.

---

## Assessment

Assessment should occur naturally during learning.

Observe:

- participation,
- communication,
- confidence,
- understanding.

Avoid lengthy written assessment.

---

## Behaviour

Include regular transitions.

Alternate:

Teacher input

↓

Student activity

↓

Movement

↓

Discussion

↓

Reflection

to maintain engagement.

---

## Final Requirement

The lesson should be enjoyable, confidence-building and suitable for Foundation Year students beginning their learning journey.
