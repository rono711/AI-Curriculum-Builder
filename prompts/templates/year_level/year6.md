## Learner Profile

Students should be supported to become increasingly self-managed learners. Use age-appropriate language, examples, contexts, resources and expectations. Do not simply make a lesson harder by increasing the quantity of work.

---

## Lesson Design

Use sustained but varied lesson phases. Connect new learning to prior knowledge, clearly communicate the learning purpose, model unfamiliar processes, provide guided practice where needed and move toward meaningful application.

Balance teacher input with active student thinking, discussion, collaboration and independent work.

---

## Vocabulary and Communication

Explicitly teach important academic and subject-specific vocabulary. Explain unfamiliar terms clearly and require students to use appropriate language when discussing, explaining and recording learning.

---

## Thinking and Questioning

Provide opportunities for students to apply, analyse, infer, justify, solve problems and create. Questions should progressively increase in cognitive demand while remaining achievable for Year 6. Require reasoning rather than answers alone.

---

## Activities

Activities should be purposeful, engaging and directly connected to the learning intention. Include an appropriate balance of modelling, guided practice, partner or group interaction, practical application, independent work and reflection.

---

## Differentiation

Support students through explicit modelling, visuals, vocabulary scaffolds, worked examples, chunked instructions, strategic grouping and additional guided practice. Extend students through deeper questions, open-ended tasks, unfamiliar applications, multiple approaches, investigation and justification.

---

## Assessment

Use questioning, application tasks, work samples, self-reflection and explanations. Assessment should reveal what students understand, how they apply learning and where further support is required.

---

## Final Requirement

The lesson must be engaging, achievable, appropriately challenging and suitable for Year 6. It should build confidence, independence, disciplinary understanding and the ability to explain and apply learning.
