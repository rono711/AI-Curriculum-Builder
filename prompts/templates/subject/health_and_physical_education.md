# Subject Guidance — Health and Physical Education (HPE)

This lesson belongs to the Australian Curriculum: Health and Physical Education (HPE).

Generate engaging, curriculum-aligned learning experiences appropriate to the nominated year level.

---

# Teaching Approach

Develop students' health literacy, movement competence, wellbeing, relationships and safe participation. Students should actively use disciplinary knowledge and skills rather than only receive information.

Integrate where appropriate:

- explicit modelling,
- guided practice,
- purposeful discussion,
- practical application,
- collaboration,
- independent thinking,
- reflection.

---

# Disciplinary Practices

Provide age-appropriate opportunities for practising movement, making decisions, communicating, reflecting, applying safety strategies and evaluating health information. Make thinking visible and require students to explain ideas using appropriate evidence, representations or examples.

---

# Vocabulary

Introduce a manageable number of important subject-specific terms. For each important new term provide a clear definition, a student-friendly explanation, contextual examples and opportunities for students to reuse it.

---

# Classroom Activities

Use purposeful activities such as movement tasks, games, scenarios, role play, skill practice, discussion, personal reflection and cooperative challenges. Activities should maximise participation and directly support the curriculum outcome.

---

# Critical Thinking

Use questions that progress from recalling and describing to applying, analysing, justifying, evaluating and creating, as appropriate to the year level. Encourage students to explain how they know, compare alternatives and support claims.

---

# Differentiation

Support learners through modelling, visuals, vocabulary support, chunked instructions, worked examples, strategic grouping and guided practice. Extend learners through open-ended tasks, deeper reasoning, independent investigation, alternative approaches and unfamiliar applications.

---

# Assessment

Use practical and formative assessment. Gather evidence through observation, questioning, student explanations, products or performances, short responses, application tasks and reflection. Assess understanding and application, not recall alone.

---

# Teacher Support

Include prerequisite knowledge, likely misconceptions, useful questioning strategies, differentiation, extension opportunities, suitable resources and practical advice for checking understanding.

---

# Final Requirement

The lesson should be engaging, practical, immediately teachable and aligned with the Australian Curriculum Version 9.0. Content, vocabulary, complexity, independence and assessment must be appropriate to the nominated year level.
