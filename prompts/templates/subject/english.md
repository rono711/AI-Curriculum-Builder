# Subject Guidance — English

This lesson belongs to the Australian Curriculum: English.

Generate engaging, curriculum-aligned English learning experiences that develop students' confidence in speaking, listening, reading, viewing and writing appropriate to their year level.

---

# Teaching Approach

English should develop students as confident communicators and thoughtful readers, writers and creators based on Elaboration:
{{ELABORATION}}.

Integrate where appropriate:

- Listening
- Speaking
- Reading
- Viewing
- Writing
- Creating
- Reflecting

Students should actively use language rather than simply learn about it.

---

# Oral Language

Provide opportunities for students to:

- discuss ideas,
- ask questions,
- explain thinking,
- share experiences,
- respond respectfully to others,
- build confidence when speaking.

For Foundation Year, oral language should be a major focus.

---

# Reading

Reading activities should:

- develop comprehension,
- build vocabulary,
- encourage prediction,
- identify key ideas,
- make connections,
- develop enjoyment of reading.

Use texts appropriate for the nominated year level.

---

# Writing

Writing activities should:

- provide clear modelling,
- scaffold sentence construction,
- encourage creativity,
- reinforce grammar naturally,
- develop confidence.

For younger students, prioritise communication over perfection.

---

# Vocabulary

Introduce a small number of important vocabulary words.

For each word provide:

- definition,
- student-friendly explanation,
- classroom examples,
- opportunities to reuse the vocabulary during the lesson.

---

# Grammar

Teach grammar in context.

Where appropriate include:

- sentence structure,
- punctuation,
- word classes,
- language features.

Avoid isolated grammar drills.

---

# Literature

Where appropriate:

- explore stories,
- poems,
- picture books,
- plays,
- informative texts,
- digital texts.

Encourage students to respond personally to texts.

---

# Critical Thinking

Include opportunities for students to:

- predict,
- infer,
- compare,
- explain,
- justify,
- evaluate,
- create.

Questions should gradually increase in complexity using Bloom's Taxonomy where appropriate.

---

# Classroom Activities

Include engaging activities such as:

- shared reading,
- guided reading,
- partner discussion,
- storytelling,
- role play,
- picture prompts,
- sequencing activities,
- matching games,
- sentence building,
- drawing and labelling,
- interactive writing.

Activities should maximise student participation.

---

# Assessment

Assessment should focus on students' ability to:

- communicate ideas,
- understand texts,
- respond appropriately,
- use vocabulary,
- apply literacy skills,
- demonstrate understanding of the curriculum outcome.

Assessment should be practical and formative wherever possible.

---

# Teacher Support

Provide practical teaching advice.

Include:

- common misconceptions,
- questioning strategies,
- differentiation ideas,
- extension opportunities,
- classroom management suggestions where appropriate.

---

# Foundation Year Considerations

For Foundation Year learners:

- use short instructions,
- include movement,
- use songs and rhymes where appropriate,
- use picture books and visual supports,
- provide repetition,
- encourage oral language throughout,
- keep tasks achievable,
- celebrate success frequently.

---

# Final Requirement

The lesson should be engaging, practical, immediately teachable and fully aligned with the Australian Curriculum Version 9.0.

Learning should build students' confidence, curiosity and enjoyment of English while developing strong foundations in literacy and communication.
