# Lesson Requirements

Generate a complete lesson that follows the Explicit Instruction model.

The lesson must include the following sections.

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

Briefly describe the purpose of the lesson.

---

## 2. Learning Intention

Write one clear learning intention.

---

## 3. Success Criteria

Provide 3–5 success criteria.

Students should understand exactly what successful learning looks like.

---

## 4. Prior Knowledge

Describe what students should already know.

Identify possible misconceptions.

---

## 5. Vocabulary

List important vocabulary.

For each word provide:

- definition
- simple explanation suitable for the year level

---

## 6. Teacher Preparation

List any materials required.

Include classroom setup if necessary.

---

## 7. Lesson Sequence

### Warm-Up

5–10 minutes

Activate prior knowledge.

---

### Teacher Modelling

Explain the new concept.

Use worked examples.

Think aloud where appropriate.

---

### Guided Practice

Teacher and students work together.

Include questions.

Provide immediate feedback opportunities.

---

### Independent Practice

Students practise independently.

Include differentiation.

---

### Reflection

Students explain what they learned.

Encourage discussion.

---

### Exit Ticket

Provide one short formative assessment.

---

## 8. Differentiation

Provide:

### Support

How struggling students can be assisted.

### Extension

How advanced students can be challenged.

---

## 9. Assessment

Explain how learning will be assessed during the lesson.

Include formative assessment opportunities.

---

## 10. Teacher Notes

Provide practical teaching advice.

Mention common misconceptions.

Suggest classroom management tips where useful.

---

## Formatting Requirements

Return the lesson in clean Markdown.

Use:

- headings
- bullet lists
- numbered lists
- tables where appropriate

Avoid large blocks of text.

---

## Final Requirement

The lesson must be immediately teachable without requiring the teacher to rewrite any section.

The lesson should reflect the quality expected from a premium Australian educational publisher.
