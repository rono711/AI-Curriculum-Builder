# Pedagogical Model

This lesson must follow the principles of Explicit Instruction.

Students should always know:

- What they are learning.
- Why they are learning it.
- How they will know they have been successful.

---

## Lesson Structure

Follow this sequence.

1. Learning Intention

Clearly explain what students will learn.

2. Success Criteria

Explain what students should be able to do by the end of the lesson.

3. Warm-up

Activate prior knowledge.

Review previously learned concepts.

4. Teacher Modelling

Introduce the new concept using clear explanations.

Model thinking aloud.

Demonstrate worked examples.

Avoid assuming prior knowledge.

5. Guided Practice

Provide supported activities.

Gradually release responsibility to students.

Use questioning throughout.

Check understanding frequently.

6. Independent Practice

Provide opportunities for students to apply learning independently.

Include differentiated practice where appropriate.

7. Review

Summarise key learning.

Revisit the Learning Intention and Success Criteria.

Address common misconceptions.

8. Exit Check

Finish with a short formative assessment to confirm understanding.

---

## Teaching Expectations

Use:

- clear explanations,
- simple language,
- step-by-step instruction,
- frequent checks for understanding,
- positive reinforcement.

Avoid long blocks of uninterrupted explanation.

Alternate teacher input with student participation.

---

## Questioning

Include questions that encourage students to:

- recall,
- explain,
- compare,
- justify,
- apply.

Where appropriate, progressively move through Bloom's Taxonomy.

---

## Differentiation

Provide support for students requiring additional assistance.

Include extension opportunities for advanced learners.

Ensure all students can successfully participate.

---

## Classroom Engagement

Use activities that encourage:

- discussion,
- collaboration,
- thinking,
- reflection,
- participation.

Students should actively construct understanding rather than passively receive information.

---

## Final Requirement

The lesson should be practical, engaging, curriculum-aligned, and immediately teachable by a classroom teacher.
