# Accessibility Requirements

All educational content must be designed to maximise accessibility, readability, and inclusion for diverse learners.

---

## Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

Where appropriate, provide multiple ways for students to:

- engage with learning,
- understand concepts,
- demonstrate understanding.

Support different learning styles through varied explanations, examples, and activities.

---

## Language

Always:

- use clear Australian English,
- write age-appropriate sentences,
- explain unfamiliar vocabulary,
- avoid unnecessary jargon,
- introduce new terminology gradually.

---

## Readability

Use:

- meaningful headings,
- short paragraphs,
- numbered steps,
- bullet lists,
- tables where appropriate.

Avoid large blocks of uninterrupted text.

---

## Cognitive Load

Reduce unnecessary cognitive load.

Present information in logical steps.

Introduce only one major concept at a time before extending learning.

Provide worked examples before independent practice.

---

## Visual Accessibility

For presentations and visual resources:

- use high-contrast colour combinations,
- avoid relying on colour alone to convey meaning,
- keep slide layouts uncluttered,
- use meaningful diagrams rather than decorative graphics,
- minimise text on presentation slides.

---

## Inclusive Learning

Ensure learning activities are inclusive.

Avoid assumptions about:

- culture,
- religion,
- gender,
- family structure,
- socioeconomic background,
- physical ability.

Use diverse examples and contexts where appropriate.

---

## Differentiation

Provide opportunities for:

### Support

Students requiring additional assistance.

Examples:

- scaffolded questions,
- sentence starters,
- worked examples,
- guided practice,
- visual supports.

### Extension

Students requiring greater challenge.

Examples:

- open-ended investigations,
- higher-order thinking,
- independent inquiry,
- real-world applications.

---

## Assessment Accessibility

Assessment tasks should:

- clearly explain expectations,
- allow students to demonstrate understanding,
- avoid unnecessary language complexity,
- focus on curriculum achievement rather than reading difficulty.

---

## Teacher Support

Where appropriate, include suggestions for:

- classroom adjustments,
- additional scaffolding,
- extension opportunities,
- alternative representations of information.

---

## AI Output Requirements

Every AI-generated resource should be:

- easy to read,
- logically organised,
- inclusive,
- accessible,
- practical for classroom use,
- suitable for the nominated year level.

---

## Final Requirement

Every lesson should reflect best practice in accessibility while remaining engaging, practical, and achievable within a normal Australian classroom.
